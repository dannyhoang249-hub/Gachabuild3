## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Fushu serves as a **support-healer hybrid**, providing both sustained HP recovery and Sanity regeneration through [Yunchi]. She excels in Hydro or multi-elemental comps focusing on uptime and sustain.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Azure Lattice (Whipsword)** – Increases Healing and Hydro DMG.  
- **Tranquil Bow** – Boosts ATK SPD and Sanity recovery effects.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Hydro Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** Healer Catalyst ×2 + Hydro Resonance ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Healing Bonus %  
2. Hydro ATK %  
3. Skill DMG %  
4. Sanity Recovery Rate  
5. Skill Duration

### Demon Wedges
- Prioritize Skill Speed boosts and Healing multipliers.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Provides teamwide Sanity and HP recovery.  
- Enhances Skill SPD and DMG through stacking.  
- High synergy with energy-hungry teams.  

**Weaknesses:**  
- Low burst damage.  
- Requires positioning to maximize [Yunchi] healing and range.
