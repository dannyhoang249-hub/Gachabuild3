### Skill — DMG · On a Warm Afternoon
Deals Lumino DMG to enemies in front and reduces their Movement Speed.

### Ultimate — Buff · Let the Tea Party Begin!
Creates a [Boisterous Tea Party] field in front of Fina. The field continuously consumes Sanity and ends when Sanity reaches 0 or the skill is held again.

**[Boisterous Tea Party]:** Generates an elemental field that changes between [Nightingale] (Hydro), [Hound] (Pyro), [Unicorn] (Electro), and [Butterfly] (Anemo). When the field appears or shifts, Fina and allies gain bonus elemental DMG matching the field type. The field also deals periodic elemental DMG every 2 seconds.

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Fina thrives as a **hybrid support** who boosts team DMG through field buffs and stacking mechanics. She fits best in teams with mixed elemental DPS.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Radiant Vow (Sword)** – Boosts Lumino DMG and teamwide buff uptime.  
- **Celestial Burst Rifle** – Increases CRIT Rate and Skill Duration.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Lumino Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** Support Catalyst ×2 + Lumino Resonance ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Skill Duration %  
2. Lumino ATK %  
3. CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG  
4. Skill Range %  
5. Resolve %

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on maintaining [Boisterous Tea Party] uptime and increasing [Taleweaver Quill] generation.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Scales with long-term field uptime and stack management.  
- Provides both crowd control and team buffs.  
- Strong adaptability with multiple elemental synergies.  

**Weaknesses:**  
- Requires sustained field presence to reach full potential.  
- Dependent on precise field repositioning timing.
