## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Berenica excels when paired with other Umbro DPS or Support units capable of extending buff duration and sanity sustain.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Ymir's Edge (Sword)** – Maximizes her combo efficiency and sanity recovery.  
- **Dual Abyss Pistols** – Good ranged fallback with fast charge.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Umbro Amplifier ×4  
- **Set 2:** Dual Phantom Hunter ×2 + Umbro Amplifier ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Umbro ATK %  
2. Skill DMG %  
3. ATK %  
4. Crit Rate / Crit DMG  
5. Sanity Efficiency

### Demon Wedges
- **Core:** Enhance Swordwave DMG and Sanity Recovery Rate.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- High burst combo potential.  
- Great synergy with Umbro-based weapon users.  
- Sustained DPS via [Ymir] state.

**Weaknesses:**  
- High Sanity consumption.  
- Vulnerable when not in [Netherflames] state.
