### Ultimate — DMG · Heaven-Earth Divination
When Zhiliu has fewer than 2 [Talismans], deals Electro DMG to enemies within range. When she holds 2 [Talismans], the skill changes based on their types:

- **[Thunder's Oracle Peal]:** 2 [Zhen] Talismans. Consumes all Talismans to deal powerful line Electro DMG.
- **[Myriad Things Thrive]:** 1 [Zhen] + 1 [Xun]. Consumes Talismans to deal periodic Electro DMG, creating [Four Realms Align] field that triggers [Zhen-Xun Convergence] every 3s.
- **[Fortune's Clarity]:** 2 [Xun] Talismans. Consumes Talismans to deal AOE Electro DMG and increase Skill DMG.

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Zhiliu excels as a flexible Electro DPS and support hybrid, alternating between offensive bursts and ally buffs through her [Talisman] mechanics.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Stormblade (Greatsword)** – Increases Electro Skill DMG and improves shield strength.  
- **Vortex Pistols** – Boost CRIT Rate and Skill Range.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Electro Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** Skill Efficiency ×2 + Electro Resonance ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Skill DMG %  
2. Electro ATK %  
3. CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG  
4. Morale  
5. Skill Duration

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on boosting Talisman retention chance and Skill DMG burst power.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Adaptive skill forms based on Talisman combinations.  
- Strong AOE and buff synergy.  
- Excellent late-battle scaling.

**Weaknesses:**  
- Requires proper Talisman rotation management.  
- Long skill animations can hinder mobility.
