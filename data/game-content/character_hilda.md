Commands all [Dirtexpellers] to revolve around Hilda, firing [Hyperthermal Purgejets]. The duration of all [Light Dirtexpellers] is paused while active.

### Ultimate — Summon · Wholehearted Service
Deals Pyro DMG to surrounding enemies and inflicts [Disarm]. For each enemy hit, summons 1 [Light Dirtexpeller] and resets all [Light Dirtexpeller] durations.

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Hilda excels as a **Pyro sub-DPS** with strong AOE potential and semi-autonomous summons. Best used with energy-support or DEF-buffer allies.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Molten Repeater** – Boosts Multi-Shot probability and Skill DMG.  
- **Twin Ember Blades** – Improves CRIT Rate and rotation speed for [Dirtexpellers].

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Pyro Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** ATK ×2 + Pyro Resonance ×2

### Stat Priority
1. ATK %  
2. Skill DMG %  
3. CRIT DMG / CRIT Rate  
4. Skill Duration %  
5. PEN %

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on maximizing projectile uptime and Pyro DMG scaling.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Excellent sustained AOE DMG.  
- Self-sufficient due to auto-targeting summons.  
- High scaling with Multi-Shot builds.  

**Weaknesses:**  
- Requires positioning for optimal [Dirtexpeller] targeting.  
- Moderate Sanity drain in continuous combat.
