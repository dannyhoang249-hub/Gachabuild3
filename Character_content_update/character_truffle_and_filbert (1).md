## Skills
### Skill — DMG · Trotters Trot-Trot
Commands [Filbert] to charge forward, dealing Anemo Damage to enemies along the path. Upon colliding with obstacles, deals AoE Anemo Damage. Heals Truffle, Filbert, and nearby allies while increasing their damage. When mounted, this skill changes to [Trotters Charge-Charge].

[Trotters Charge-Charge]: Hold to perform a sustained charge, consuming Sanity over time and dealing Anemo Damage continuously. Colliding with an obstacle ends the charge, releasing AoE Anemo Damage.

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Truffle and Filbert excel as a mobile support-healer duo that synergizes with Anemo or HP-scaling allies. Ideal for team compositions needing sustain and AoE crowd control.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Windtamer Lance** – Boosts Anemo DMG and Healing.  
- **Celestial Harp (Bow)** – Enhances Healing output and Sanity regeneration.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Anemo Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** Healing Harmony ×2 + Anemo Resonance ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Max HP %  
2. Healing Bonus %  
3. Skill Duration  
4. Sanity Efficiency  
5. DEF %

### Demon Wedges
- Prioritize improving healing scaling and [Trotters Charge-Charge] AoE DMG.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Excellent mobility and team healing.  
- High survivability through HP scaling.  
- Unique mount mechanics for dynamic gameplay.

**Weaknesses:**  
- Limited ranged capabilities while mounted.  
- Requires precise positioning to maximize healing and damage.
