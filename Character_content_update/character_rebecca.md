## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Rebecca excels in Hydro-centered DoT teams, providing both summon and poison spread utility. Best paired with sustain supports and elemental amplifiers.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Tidepiercer (Polearm)** – Boosts Hydro DoT damage scaling.  
- **Aqua Bloom Launcher** – Increases summon duration and range.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Ocean Symphony ×4  
- **Set 2:** Hydro Catalyst ×2 + Ocean Symphony ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Hydro ATK %  
2. Skill DMG %  
3. ATK %  
4. Sanity Efficiency  
5. Summon Duration

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on improving [Poison of Love] spread and summon uptime.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- High DoT potential through poison transfer.  
- Excellent AoE damage scaling with summons.  
- Great synergy with Hydro resonance teams.

**Weaknesses:**  
- Requires setup time for poison stacking.  
- Vulnerable during summon downtime.
