## Skills
### Skill — DMG · Tot Popper
Drops a bomb named [Tot Popper], assuming either [Tot Popper: Yale’s Make] or [Tot Popper: Oliver’s Make]. Upon landing, it explodes after 2 seconds, dealing Pyro Damage in a cross-shaped area and detonating nearby [Tot Poppers]. Up to 10 [Tot Poppers] can exist simultaneously.

**[Tot Popper: Yale’s Make]:** Causes greater stance reduction. Higher chance to deploy when Skill Range greatly exceeds Skill Damage (≥50% difference guarantees Yale’s Make).

**[Tot Popper: Oliver’s Make]:** Deals higher damage. Higher chance to deploy when Skill Damage exceeds Skill Range (≥50% difference guarantees Oliver’s Make).

### Ultimate — DMG · Nightmare’s Grasp
Generates a [Nightmare Lab] field. Activating again closes the field early.

**[Nightmare Lab]:** Slows enemies and pauses the detonation of existing [Tot Poppers]. Upon closure, all [Tot Poppers] within detonate, causing a massive explosion that deals Pyro Damage. Each [Tot Popper] detonated increases explosion damage and restores Sanity.

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Yale and Oliver excel in AoE burst DPS roles through chain detonations. Works well with supports that increase Pyro DMG or Sanity regeneration.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Flameburst Shotgun** – Enhances AoE and explosion DMG.  
- **Inferno Saber** – Boosts Skill DMG and Pyro ATK.  

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Pyro Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** Blast Expert ×2 + Pyro Resonance ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Pyro ATK %  
2. Skill DMG %  
3. Skill Efficiency  
4. ATK %  
5. Sanity Regeneration

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on increasing explosion radius and reducing [Tot Popper] detonation delay.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Strong AoE Pyro burst potential.  
- High utility with dual bomb mechanics.  
- Sanity sustain through detonation synergy.  

**Weaknesses:**  
- Requires field setup and timing for maximum impact.  
- Limited mobility during [Nightmare Lab].
