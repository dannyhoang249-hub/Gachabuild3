### Ultimate — DMG · Margie’s Combustible Bin
Casts 3 [Margie’s Combustible Bins] forward. Each explodes upon hitting a target or obstacle, dealing AOE Pyro DMG and consuming [Jackpot!] stacks.

- **3 stacks:** Increases explosion range and damage.  
- **6 stacks:** Creates a Zealous Zone that deals Pyro DMG every 2s.  
- **9 stacks:** Adds 2 additional AOE Pyro explosions after the main blast.

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Margie serves as a **Pyro control-DPS** capable of crowd control and stacking scaling buffs. Ideal for combo setups with energy supports or area denial teams.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Blazing Excavator** – Boosts continuous Skill DMG and AoE radius.  
- **Inferno Harvester** – Increases ATK SPD and Sanity recovery.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Pyro Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** Skill Duration ×2 + Pyro Resonance ×2

### Stat Priority
1. ATK %  
2. Skill DMG %  
3. CRIT DMG / CRIT Rate  
4. Skill Duration %  
5. PEN %

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on maximizing [Jackpot!] generation and AoE scaling.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Flexible scaling via permanent [Jackpot!] stacks.  
- Strong AoE control and stun utility.  
- Excellent synergy with drop-based or resource-heavy teams.  

**Weaknesses:**  
- Requires loot pickup or ally synergy to ramp up.  
- Short-range attacks can expose her to enemy retaliation.
