## Skills
### Skill — Healing · Emerald Effusion
Grants [Emerald Effusion]. While active, alchemical smoke fills the area around Daphne, continuously healing self and allies while slowing enemies in the area.  
While [Emerald Effusion] is active, generates 2 [Azoth Projectiles] per second.

### Ultimate — Buff · Repulsion Synthesis
Heals self and allies, applying [Purification Formula]. Briefly restrains nearby enemies and inflicts [Decay Formula] on them.  
Generates 1 [Azoth Projectile] per affected target (ally or enemy), up to 12.

[Purification Formula]: Gains bonus Anemo Damage.  
[Decay Formula]: Decreases Attack and deals periodic Anemo Damage to affected targets once every 2s.

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Daphne shines in compositions that need sustained healing and control. Best paired with Anemo DPS or teammates who trigger formula effects.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Azure Lash (Whipsword)** – Enhances healing efficiency.  
- **Harmonic Pulse Rifle** – Boosts ranged Anemo trigger synergy.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Vital Stream ×4  
- **Set 2:** Anemo Conductor ×2 + Vital Stream ×2

### Stat Priority
1. ATK %  
2. Healing Bonus %  
3. Skill Duration  
4. CRIT Chance / CRIT DMG  
5. Sanity Efficiency

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on increasing [Azoth Projectile] storage and recovery rate.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Consistent healing and team-wide buffs.  
- Dual-purpose control and sustain.  
- Generates Azoth ammo to maintain uptime.

**Weaknesses:**  
- High Sanity consumption during extended rotations.  
- Limited burst healing potential without Ultimate.
