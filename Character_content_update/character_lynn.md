## Skills
### Skill — DMG · Deadly Blossom
Deals Pyro Damage to enemies in front and inflicts [Laceration].  

[Laceration]: When under this effect, every time the enemy takes ranged weapon damage, the [Lacerated Wound] count increases by 1. When the duration ends, deals Pyro Damage equal to the [Lacerated Wound] count × [Laceration DMG] once.

### Ultimate — Buff · Gunpowder Revelry
Switches the ranged weapon to [Hansel and Gretel]. In this state, Lynn can shoot and use skills but can only move using [Deadly Blossom].  
Shooting deals damage to all enemies in front, during which Lynn gains 1 stack that increases ATK Speed every 0.2s (up to 35 stacks). Using [Hansel and Gretel] continuously consumes Sanity, during which Lynn is immune to Hitstun. When Sanity drops to 0 or the skill is reused, Lynn sheathes [Hansel and Gretel].

### Passive — Tactical Rapid Fire
When not shooting, Lynn gains 1 stack of [Preload] per second, up to 6 stacks. When shooting, consumes all [Preload] stacks to gain [Suppressing Fire] for a duration.  
[Suppressing Fire]: Increases damage based on the number of consumed [Preload] stacks. Cannot gain [Preload] stacks during [Suppressing Fire].

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Lynn excels in aggressive Pyro-based compositions with teammates that can sustain or extend combo windows.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Hansel & Gretel (Dual Pistols)** – Optimized for ATK Speed scaling and bonus Pyro damage.  
- **Molten Greatblade** – Ideal for hybrid melee-ranged setups.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Crimson Pyre ×4  
- **Set 2:** Ember Vanguard ×2 + Crimson Pyre ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Pyro ATK %  
2. ATK Speed %  
3. Skill DMG %  
4. Crit Rate / Crit DMG  
5. Sanity Efficiency

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on enhancing [Laceration] detonation damage and sanity sustain.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Excellent sustained DPS via [Gunpowder Revelry].  
- Scales well with ATK Speed and ranged buffs.  
- Hybrid flexibility between melee and ranged combat.

**Weaknesses:**  
- High Sanity consumption under extended burst.  
- Vulnerable without Preload stacks or during cooldowns.
