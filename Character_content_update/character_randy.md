## Skills
### Skill — DEF · Legitimate Slackness
Deploys a [Barrier] in front of Randy and deals Electro Damage to nearby enemies.  
[Barrier] consumes Sanity over time; Randy cannot move or use weapons but becomes immune to Hitstun. [Barrier] inflicts [Overwork Grudge] on nearby enemies.  
Using the skill again retracts the [Barrier].

[Barrier]: Blocks frontal projectiles, absorbing damage equal to 100% of Randy’s Max HP.  
[Overwork Grudge]: Decreases ATK and deals Electro Damage every 2 seconds.

### Ultimate — DMG · Special Gift
Throws a [Bespoke Shield] forward, triggering [Impact] once when it halts. [Bespoke Shield] triggers [Impact] every 3 seconds while active.  
[Impact] deals damage against target’s shield based on Randy’s DEF, granting an Ultra Shield to teammates within range. For targets without shields, [Impact] causes paralysis.

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Randy fits perfectly in defensive or hybrid support teams, protecting allies while applying consistent Electro debuffs. Best paired with Electro DPS or shield-dependent allies.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Ion Defender (Sword)** – Boosts DEF-scaling damage and Ultra Shield efficiency.  
- **Aegis Blaster (Pistol)** – Improves ranged Electro support potential.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Guardian Bastion ×4  
- **Set 2:** Bastion ×2 + Electro Conductor ×2

### Stat Priority
1. DEF %  
2. Skill Duration  
3. Sanity Efficiency  
4. ATK %  
5. Shield Strength

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on amplifying DEF-based damage and Ultra Shield output.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Reliable defensive utility and ally shielding.  
- Applies ATK debuff via [Overwork Grudge].  
- Excellent sustain and crowd control.

**Weaknesses:**  
- Immobile during [Barrier] deployment.  
- Moderate damage scaling; more of a tank-support hybrid.
