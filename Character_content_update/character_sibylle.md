## Skills
### Skill — DMG · Divine Conqueror
Deals Electro Damage to enemies within range in front and creates a field of [Legion's Punishment] at the location.

[Legion's Punishment]: After a 1.5s delay, consumes [Might of Thunder] every 0.4s to deal Electro Damage to one random enemy within range for up to 10s. When [Might of Thunder] is depleted, the field dissipates. A maximum of 3 fields can exist at once.

### Ultimate — Summon · Stormcaller
Summons [Chaotic Thunderorb]. Consumes [Might of Thunder] to enhance the summon, reducing [Devastating Storm]’s trigger interval to 2s and increasing Link Damage.

[Chaotic Thunderorb]: Inherits a portion of Sibylle’s Max HP (0 DEF), considered an enemy unit. Calls forth [Devastating Storm] every 3s and links to furthest enemies in range, up to 10. Linked targets take Electro Damage per second and share damage taken by the orb.

[Devastating Storm]: Deals Electro Damage to nearby enemies.

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Sibylle excels in HP-scaling Electro teams, focusing on sustained AoE via [Legion’s Punishment] and [Chaotic Thunderorb]. Works well with defensive or healing supports.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Thunderlash (Whipsword)** – Increases Electro DMG and HP scaling.  
- **Pulse Repeater (Pistol)** – Boosts field damage duration and Sanity recovery.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Electro Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** Vital Surge ×2 + Electro Resonance ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Max HP %  
2. Electro DMG %  
3. Skill DMG %  
4. Sanity Efficiency  
5. DEF %

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on extending [Legion’s Punishment] uptime and increasing orb damage transfer.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Extremely durable HP-scaling DPS.  
- Great AoE and sustained Electro field pressure.  
- Scales naturally with defensive builds.

**Weaknesses:**  
- Dependent on [Might of Thunder] buildup.  
- Summon placement can be disrupted by mobile enemies.
