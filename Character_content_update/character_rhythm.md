### Ultimate — Buff · Electro Vortex
Deals Electro Damage to enemies within range and enters [Electro Surge] state, continuously consuming Sanity. [Electro Surge] ends when Sanity reaches 0 or the skill is reused.

[Electro Surge]: Increases Movement Speed for Rhythm and allies, dealing Electro Damage to 1 random target per second. Alternately inflicts [Positive Charge] and [Negative Charge] on enemies, prioritizing uncharged ones.

[Electric Charge]: [Positive] and [Negative] targets are pulled together every 3 seconds, both taking Electro Damage. If no opposite charge exists, damage is dealt to a random target. Reapplying the same charge refreshes its duration.

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Rhythm excels in Electro reaction and energy loop teams. Ideal as a DPS or sub-DPS that sustains Electro debuffs while generating [Electric Energy] for teammates.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Voltfang Blade (Katana)** – Boosts Electro damage and skill uptime.  
- **Stormbreaker Rifle** – Amplifies Electro reaction DMG and Sanity recovery.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Electro Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** Electro Resonance ×2 + Energy Conversion ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Electro ATK %  
2. Skill DMG %  
3. ATK %  
4. Sanity Efficiency  
5. Movement SPD

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on increasing [Electric Charge] duration and [Volt Rush] multipliers.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Excellent Electro AoE DPS and crowd control.  
- Efficient self-sustaining [Electric Energy] loop.  
- High mobility during [Electro Surge].

**Weaknesses:**  
- High Sanity consumption in long fights.  
- Requires energy setup for full potential.
