## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Hellfire functions as a **HP-scaling Pyro DPS and tank hybrid**, excelling in sustained damage and enemy crowd control via Taunts.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Blazefury Greatsword** – Boosts HP scaling DMG and Taunt duration.  
- **Inferno Launcher** – Increases Skill DMG and extends [Retribution] duration.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Pyro Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** Max HP ×2 + Pyro Resonance ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Max HP %  
2. Pyro ATK %  
3. Skill DMG %  
4. CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG  
5. Skill Duration

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on HP scaling and [Retribution] uptime.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Extremely durable with HP regeneration and scaling DMG.  
- Strong AOE and Taunt utility.  
- Synergizes well with Pyro-focused comps.

**Weaknesses:**  
- High Sanity cost limits uptime.  
- Limited mobility during [Retribution] state.
