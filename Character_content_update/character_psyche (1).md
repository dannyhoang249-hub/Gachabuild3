## Skills
### Skill — DMG · Chrysalis Dreamweave
Creates 1 [Phantasm] and flashes forward. Can be used anytime without interrupting Psyche’s current action. In [Flamboyance] state, replaced with [Phototaxis].

**[Phantasm]:** Continuously slows nearby enemies, then after 5s deals Anemo DMG and vanishes. Creating a new [Phantasm] instantly detonates the previous one.

**[Phototaxis]:** Dashes forward, releasing 16 [Ethereal Powders] that lock onto enemies, dealing Anemo DMG and inflicting [Powder Stigma] (max 12 stacks).

**[Powder Stigma]:** When targets take ranged weapon DMG from Psyche, 1 stack is consumed to deal Anemo DMG.

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Psyche excels as an aerial DPS focusing on sustained Anemo damage and CRIT synergy. Best paired with characters who can maintain buffs during her [Flamboyance] uptime.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Zephyr’s Spear (Polearm)** – Boosts Anemo DMG and airborne attack potency.  
- **Tempest Rifle** – Increases CRIT Rate and Skill Range.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Anemo Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** CRIT Catalyst ×2 + Anemo Resonance ×2

### Stat Priority
1. CRIT DMG / CRIT Rate  
2. Anemo ATK %  
3. Skill DMG %  
4. Resolve  
5. ATK %

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on extending [Flamboyance] duration and increasing [Powder Stigma] damage.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Excellent mobility and ranged burst.  
- Strong CRIT-based scaling.  
- Self-healing and shield conversion during flight.  

**Weaknesses:**  
- HP drain mechanic can be risky without sustain.  
- Requires good timing to manage buffs and stacks effectively.
