### Ultimate — Buff · Lightswords Slice Starry Streams
Enters [Aspect Manifestation] state, summoning [Edges of All Times] to rain from the sky and deal Lumino DMG.  
During this state, Melee Weapon becomes [Phanosword], which gains extended range and alternate attack modes. Triggering Bonus Effects generates [Phanoplumes].  
While in [Aspect Manifestation], this skill becomes [Feather Chase Dust in Dreams].

**[Phanoplume]:** Homes onto enemies, dealing Lumino DMG; max 7 can exist.  
**[Feathers Chase Dust in Dreams]:** Ends [Aspect Manifestation], dealing high Lumino DMG to enemies in front.

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Kezhou operates as a **hybrid DPS** capable of switching between melee and ranged combat. His dual-intent system allows flexible scaling in hybrid builds.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Phanosword Prototype** – Boosts Intent charge rate and Melee DMG.  
- **Starlight Blaster** – Reduces Sanity cost and enhances ranged attack scaling.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Lumino Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** Dual Balance ×2 + Lumino Resonance ×2

### Stat Priority
1. ATK %  
2. Lumino DMG %  
3. CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG  
4. Skill Duration  
5. PEN %

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on Intent generation and Sanity regeneration.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Highly adaptable hybrid kit.  
- Powerful scaling through Intent system.  
- High AOE potential with [Edges of All Times].  

**Weaknesses:**  
- Complex management of Intent mechanics.  
- High Sanity cost for sustained DPS.
