## Skills
### Skill — Buff · Precipitous Thunderfall
Yuming leaps into the air, replacing his next Plunging Attack with [Starlight Descent]. When used in [Xuanjiao Manifest] state, gains 1 [Reverse Scale]. At maximum [Reverse Scales], [Starlight Descent] is enhanced into [Starstorm Descent].

**[Starlight Descent]:** Plunges downward, dealing AOE Electro DMG. Triggers [Wyrmcall Clouds] when in [Xuanjiao Manifest].

**[Reverse Scale]:** Stacks up to 6 and are cleared when [Xuanjiao Manifest] ends.

**[Wyrmcall Clouds]:** Unleashes 5 [Raging Thunder] strikes around the target, prioritizing different locations.

**[Starstorm Descent]:** Consumes all [Reverse Scales], granting Skill DMG boosts to Yuming and nearby allies, dealing massive Electro DMG.

### Ultimate — Buff · Grimscales
Deals Electro DMG to enemies in front and enters [Xuanjiao Manifest] state.

**[Xuanjiao Manifest]:** Grants Hitstun immunity. Xuanjiao hovers around Yuming, dealing periodic Electro DMG to nearby enemies (every 2s). When Yuming or allies consume Sanity to cast a skill, their next Skill DMG triggers [Fish-Dragon Dance], striking AOE Electro DMG. Xuanjiao absorbs a portion of Yuming’s damage; once damage absorption exceeds a threshold, the state ends.

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Yuming functions as a high-skill Electro burst DPS focusing on plunge attacks and synergy with Sanity management allies.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Thunderpiercer (Polearm)** – Boosts Electro ATK and plunging DMG.  
- **Skybreaker Rifle** – Improves Skill DMG and CRIT stats.  

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Electro Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** CRIT Catalyst ×2 + Electro Resonance ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Electro ATK %  
2. Skill DMG %  
3. CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG  
4. Skill Duration  
5. ATK %

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on enhancing plunge DMG multipliers and Sanity restoration rate.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Exceptional burst potential through [Starstorm Descent].  
- High synergy with skill-based team comps.  
- Provides teamwide buffs from Reverse Scale mechanics.  

**Weaknesses:**  
- Requires precise timing to maintain Reverse Scales.  
- Dependent on uptime of [Xuanjiao Manifest].
