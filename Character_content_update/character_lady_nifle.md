### Ultimate — Buff · Misty Sea Requiem
Enters [Funeral Rite] state. If Skill Range > 100%, the excess converts to Skill DMG and range is reset to 100%.  
While under [Funeral Rite]:
- Ranged weapons gain Trigger Probability boost.  
- [Lunar Hunt] & [Solar Eclipse] cost no Sanity.  
- [Lunar Hunt] pulls enemies inward.  
- [Solar Eclipse] deals enhanced DMG.  
Ends after 12s or after casting 6 total [Lunar Hunt]/[Solar Eclipse].

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Lady Nifle is a **Lumino burst DPS** who alternates between Umbro and Lumino attacks. Excels in AOE control and alternating damage cycles.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Lunar Reverence** – Enhances Skill Range and Lumino DMG.  
- **Solar Oath** – Boosts Trigger Probability and ultimate damage.  

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Lumino Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** Skill Range ×2 + Lumino Resonance ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Lumino DMG %  
2. Skill DMG %  
3. CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG  
4. Skill Range %  
5. Trigger Probability %

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on enhancing [Interwoven Cries] proc rate and maintaining [Funeral Rite] uptime.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Dual-element versatility (Umbro + Lumino).  
- Massive burst damage under [Funeral Rite].  
- High AOE coverage and CC utility.

**Weaknesses:**  
- Dependent on cooldown cycling between skills.  
- Requires precision timing to maximize dual-skill chaining.
