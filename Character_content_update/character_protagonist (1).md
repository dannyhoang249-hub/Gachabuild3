## Build Guide
### Role Overview
The Protagonist functions as a **Lumino hybrid Support-DPS**, combining crowd control, buff uptime, and team sustain. [Flare] offers reliable shielding and Lumino synergy for resonance-based compositions.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Dawnkeeper Blade** – Boosts Skill DMG and Lumino amplification.  
- **Radiant Alloy Rifle** – Enhances Trigger Probability and Ultra Shield generation.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Lumino Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** Lumino Resonance ×2 + Shield Booster ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Lumino DMG %  
2. Skill Duration %  
3. CRIT DMG / CRIT Rate  
4. ATK %  
5. Trigger Probability %

### Team Recommendations
- Works best as **sub-DPS or support hybrid** alongside burst Lumino characters like Lisbell or Lady Nifle.  
- Excellent in teams that benefit from weapon damage amplification and sustained shielding.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Team-wide shielding and DMG buff synergy.  
- Simple skill rotation ideal for beginners.  
- Reliable bonus damage through passive [Diffusion].  

**Weaknesses:**  
- Limited burst compared to pure DPS units.  
- Buff duration requires consistent skill cycling.
