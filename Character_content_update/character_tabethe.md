## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Tabethe is an excellent Hydro support-summoner. She provides sustained crowd control through her tentacles while boosting Skill DMG for the entire team.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Oceanstring Bow** – Increases Summon DMG and CRIT Chance.  
- **Foamblade (Sword)** – Grants bonus Hydro DMG and boosts [Tentacle] uptime.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Hydro Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** Hydro Resonance ×2 + Summoner’s Insight ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Hydro ATK %  
2. Summon DMG %  
3. CRIT Chance / CRIT DMG  
4. Skill Duration  
5. ATK %

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on increasing tentacle duration and Hydro AoE radius.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- Exceptional crowd control and area lockdown.  
- Reliable Hydro summon uptime.  
- Strong scaling through CRIT synergy.

**Weaknesses:**  
- Tentacle positioning can miss moving targets.  
- Limited burst potential compared to pure DPS roles.
