## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Lisbell excels as a **melee Consonance DPS**, scaling through Combo Levels and charged attacks.  
She fits well in Lumino-heavy teams for resonance buffs.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Samael’s Oath** – Enhances Charged Attack DMG and Combo Duration.  
- **Radiant Crusader** – Increases Skill Duration and boosts Lumino Resonance.  

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Lumino Resonance ×4  
- **Set 2:** Combo Burst ×2 + Lumino Resonance ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Lumino DMG %  
2. ATK %  
3. CRIT DMG / CRIT Rate  
4. Skill Duration  
5. PEN %

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on increasing Combo generation and maintaining [Impatience] uptime.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- High single-target burst with Combo scaling.  
- Excellent synergy with Consonance weapons.  
- Consistent Lumino DMG output.

**Weaknesses:**  
- High Sanity consumption for sustained charge.  
- Requires timing to maintain Combo chain.
