## Skills
### Skill — DMG · Recitativo
Deals Umbro Damage to enemies in front and inflicts 5 stacks of [Adagio] on them, up to 10 stacks. The remaining duration is refreshed with each new stack. When in [Bestial Fury] state, this skill is replaced with [Aria].

[Aria]: Dashes forward, dealing Umbro Damage to enemies along the path, and gains [Presto].  
[Presto]: Increases Skill Range and grants immunity to Hitstun.

### Ultimate — Buff · Curtain Up
Enters [Bestial Fury] state and continuously consumes Sanity; when Sanity drops to 0, the state ends.  
Melee Attack is replaced with [Furious Claws], performing up to four consecutive attacks to deal Umbro Damage. When used in air, performs Plunging Attack dealing AoE Damage. Ranged Attack becomes [Mournful Howl], firing an energy beam.  

When the 3rd hit of [Furious Claws] or [Mournful Howl] deals damage, triggers [Duo]; when the 4th hit or Plunging Attack deals damage, triggers [Fermata].  

[Duo]: Grants a certain amount of [Immersion]. Can trigger once every 0.1s.  
[Fermata]: Increases damage based on target’s [Adagio] stacks, removes all stacks, restores Sanity if 10 stacks exist, and grants [Immersion].

### Passive — On Stage
When in [Bestial Fury], increases Attack based on Max Sanity. Gains [Immersion] by using skills, capped at Max Sanity. Using [Recitativo] or [Aria] grants [Immersion]; [Bestial Fury] continuously grants [Immersion] per second. When maxed, enters [Bestial Fury: Devotion].

[Bestial Fury: Devotion]: Consumes [Immersion] instead of Sanity to sustain [Bestial Fury], increasing Movement Speed, Skill Speed, and ATK based on Max Sanity. Prevents [Immersion] gain while active.

## Build Guide
### Team Composition
Phantasio excels in fast-paced Umbro teams with other damage dealers or sanity regenerators. His [Bestial Fury] thrives on synergy with buffers and sustain units.

### Recommended Weapons
- **Feral Sonata (Greatsword)** – Boosts Umbro Skill DMG scaling with Sanity.  
- **Dark Resonance Shotgun** – Enhances [Mournful Howl] efficiency.

### Recommended Artifacts
- **Set 1:** Umbro Conductor ×4  
- **Set 2:** Abyssal Echo ×2 + Umbro Conductor ×2

### Stat Priority
1. Umbro ATK %  
2. Skill DMG %  
3. ATK %  
4. Sanity Efficiency  
5. CRIT DMG / CRIT Rate

### Demon Wedges
- Focus on boosting [Immersion] gain and [Bestial Fury] duration.

### Pros & Cons
**Strengths:**  
- High scaling burst and transformation utility.  
- Excellent synergy with sanity-based builds.  
- Can sustain DPS through [Devotion] mode.

**Weaknesses:**  
- Heavy sanity drain.  
- Requires good timing to optimize [Adagio] stacks and [Immersion].
